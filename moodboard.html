pablo picasso · the bull · 1945-1946 · blackboard starting point page

Picasso's Le Taureau (The Bull) is an example of how an artist can take something that exists in real life, like a bull, and abstract a drawing of that object down to the simplest form possible.

This drawing in particular is inspirational because it shows the kind of stripping back layer by layer that I want to do with my object.

todd mclellan · things come apart

Todd's work here provides a perspective that most people don't get to see, the inner workings of electronic devices. This example, from an iPod, illustrates this by showing the user all the electronic and metal parts that goes into a device like an iPod.

fabian oefner · the heisenberg series

The Heisenberg Series is a collection of sculptures by Fabian Oefner. It involved submerging an object in resin, then chopping it up. The result is an abstract work of art that reveals the inner works of the device.
