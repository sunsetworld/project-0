Welcome to project 0. This project shows the inner workings of a fictional portable games console.

This project is designed to inform you about what goes into a portable games console.

Electronics have an enormous environmental and social footprint, yet this is something that most users of these devices don't see,

because the process of making them, powering them and disposing of them is largely invisible to the end user.

Hopefully, this guide will give you a better idea of whats in your electronics.
